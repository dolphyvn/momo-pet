# EPIC-008 — Watch App & Sync

## Objective
Complete the FIRST VERTICAL SLICE: iPhone state reaches the Watch, and an offline Watch pat applies exactly once on the iPhone. MomoWatch leaves its placeholder shell and becomes **W1** — the one glanceable surface (mood in words + bond stage word + the single quest line + the pat) rendering the last-synced `WatchSnapshot` instantly from local storage — while the iPhone-side WC session pushes a latest-wins context on every state change and applies journaled pats exactly once. The Watch runs **no engine** (ADR-003): it renders iPhone-issued snapshots through the shared pure derivations and queues pat intents; the iPhone stays authoritative (D5/§21).

## User / Product Value
The Watch is Momo's independent companion surface (§2, §12, §44; PR6 — "independent value, not a phone mirror"): raise the wrist, see how Momo feels in words, pat from anywhere — offline, iPhone away, whatever — and the affection lands exactly once when the phone returns. No guilt copy, no freshness indicator, no sync error ever exists (UX-9; FR-18 AC-4). This closes the delivery plan's spine (§4.2): "Watch receives state" (TASK-040 + TASK-041) — and with it the complete first vertical slice.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. The iPhone pushes a latest-wins context on every state change through the `.pushWatchSnapshot` seam; the plan core's fixed side-effect order is preserved and the seam remains the ONLY new engine exit (D-R5); the context carries DisplayState, quest inputs, the haptics flag, and the epoch-scoped watermark.
2. Watch-originated intents apply EXACTLY ONCE on the iPhone: fold-to-now → unseen-UUID ∧ strictly-greater-watermark (per-epoch, 0-init) → next snapshot carries the watermark; duplicates, replays, and redeliveries are no-ops (INV-10); hello is device-agnostic against the intent's own `dayKey`; expired-dayKey intents apply current-state effects with day attribution dropped.
3. W1 renders the last-synced snapshot instantly from local storage (persisted on every receive + background transition), with mood/stage in words and the single quest line — no numbers, no bars, no freshness indicator, no error surface, ever; the settling-in line shows pre-first-sync.
4. The Watch pat is fully offline-first: immediate micro-reaction + toggle-honoring haptic (state-distinct; asleep = stir + heart, stays asleep), intent journaled with epoch + monotonic seq, drained reliably; raise-to-pat ≤ 5 s; haptics follow UX §6.3 exactly (nothing outside pat + on-wrist completion).
5. The Watch runs no engine: the quest line is the shared cascade output; all bond/counters/quests move only on the iPhone; bond cannot regress on either device (INV-3); the haptics toggle travels via snapshot; no Watch settings surface exists.
6. Reset flows hold end-to-end: erase sets the reset marker in the next context → the Watch wipes snapshot + journal at next sync → settling-in line (TASK-038's AC-2 completes); epoch re-initialization (re-pair/reinstall/new Watch) never starves pats; a stale-epoch watermark never prunes a newer journal (INV-10).
7. AOD pauses all looping animation behind the static glyph (FR-17 AC-5); the glyph reads correctly at true 24–32 pt on W1/AOD (O4 closed); restore ≤ ~2 s asserted in the Watch UI suite.
8. Full sync suites green including the §10.4 matrix; the paired-device obligation is executed on hardware or BLOCKED recorded (no simulator-only claim — §25).
